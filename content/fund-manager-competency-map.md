# Fund Manager Competency Map

## Core Competencies

- Research depth: understand business models, industries, and competitive dynamics
- Financial fluency: read statements, detect quality, and connect accounting to economics
- Valuation judgment: estimate value ranges and identify expectations embedded in price
- Portfolio construction: size positions, manage concentration, and balance conviction with risk
- Risk management: think in scenarios, downside, and thesis break conditions
- Communication: write clear memos and defend decisions under scrutiny
- Process discipline: review outcomes without confusing process quality with short-term results
