# Investment Memo Template

## Section Order

1. Business overview
2. Why it matters
3. Revenue and unit economics
4. Financial quality
5. Valuation view
6. Key risks
7. Variant perception or edge
8. Position conclusion
9. Follow-up questions

## Prompted Template

### 1. Business overview

- What does the company do?
- How does it make money?
- Which segment actually matters?

### 2. Why it matters

- Why is this business worth studying now?
- What is mispriced, ignored, or misunderstood?

### 3. Revenue and unit economics

- What drives revenue growth?
- What are the key margins, cohorts, or unit economics?
- What operating leverage exists?

### 4. Financial quality

- Does profit convert into cash?
- What does the balance sheet say about resilience?
- Are there accounting concerns?

### 5. Valuation view

- What is the stock pricing in today?
- What is a reasonable value range?
- Which assumptions matter most?

### 6. Key risks

- What breaks the thesis?
- What is outside your circle of competence?
- What can go wrong operationally, financially, or competitively?

### 7. Variant perception or edge

- What do you believe that consensus may be missing?
- Why do you have a right to that view?

### 8. Position conclusion

- Buy, watch, or reject?
- What would make conviction increase or decrease?
- What position size would fit a real portfolio?

### 9. Follow-up questions

- What still needs evidence?
- Which management, industry, or accounting questions are unresolved?

## Writing Rule

- Keep claims tied to evidence
- Separate facts, assumptions, and judgment
- State what would invalidate the thesis
- Keep the conclusion actionable
