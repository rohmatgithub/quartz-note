# Investment Theses & Stock Pitches

This area links theory to visible research output.

## Good Pitch Characteristics

- Clear variant perception
- Business understanding
- Financial logic
- Valuation support
- Explicit risks
- Actionable conclusion

## Pitch Standard

- The thesis should be understandable in a few sentences
- The business should be simple enough for you to explain clearly
- The valuation should support the upside without heroic assumptions
- The risks should be real, specific, and uncomfortable
- The conclusion should reflect portfolio reality, not classroom theory

## What Makes A Thesis Strong

- It connects business quality to valuation
- It identifies a concrete source of mispricing
- It distinguishes fact from interpretation
- It includes disconfirming evidence
- It states what would change your mind
