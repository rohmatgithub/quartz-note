# Accounting & Financial Statements

This is a priority area because valuation quality depends on understanding the underlying numbers.

## Statement Stack

- Income statement
- Balance sheet
- Cash flow statement

## What To Learn First

- How revenue becomes earnings
- Why cash flow can diverge from reported profit
- How working capital affects business quality
- Which balance-sheet items create hidden fragility
- How management can distort true economics

## High-Value Concepts

- Working capital
- Earnings quality
- Red flags and distortions
- Capital intensity
- Margins and operating leverage
- Return on invested capital

## Review Checklist

- Does revenue growth convert into cash?
- Are margins stable, expanding, or flattered?
- Is working capital consuming or releasing cash?
- Are leverage and liquidity appropriate for the business?
- Do adjustments make earnings look better than reality?

## Common Red Flags

- Profit growth with weak operating cash flow
- Frequent non-recurring adjustments
- Rising receivables without matching business explanation
- Inventory growth that outpaces demand
- Debt dependence masked by optimistic narratives
