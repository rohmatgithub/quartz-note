# Reading & Curriculum

## Primary Study Track

- [[six-month-technical-syllabus|6-Month Technical Syllabus]]

## Core Curriculum

- Accounting and financial statements
- Valuation methods
- Market history and investor psychology
- Portfolio management and risk
- Business strategy and industry structure
- Shareholder letters, annual reports, and earnings transcripts

## Resource Buckets

- Books
- Investor letters
- Company filings
- Earnings calls
- Courses
- Internal notes and summaries

## Study Rule

Every major resource should generate one of the following:

- a concept note under [[topics/finance|Finance]]
- a tool or workflow idea under [[topics/learn-tech|Quant & Research Tech]]
- a practical output in [[investment-memo-template|Investment Memo Template]]

## Reading Conversion Rule

- Accounting and valuation reading should end in a written note or memo section
- Market and business reading should improve company judgment, not stay abstract
- Technical tooling study should support research speed, data quality, or review loops
