# Research Pipeline

## Pipeline Stages

1. Idea sourcing
2. Initial screen
3. Business and industry deep dive
4. Financial quality assessment
5. Valuation work
6. Risk mapping
7. Memo draft
8. Position conclusion
9. Post-decision review

## Input Sources

- Screens and watchlists
- Investor letters
- Earnings calls
- Industry news
- Personal observations
