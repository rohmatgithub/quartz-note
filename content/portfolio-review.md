# Portfolio Review

## Review Dimensions

- Thesis quality
- Entry and exit logic
- Position sizing
- Risk taken versus risk understood
- What was knowable at the time
- Mistakes in process versus mistakes in outcome

## Cadence

- Weekly watchlist review
- Monthly thesis review
- Quarterly portfolio process review
