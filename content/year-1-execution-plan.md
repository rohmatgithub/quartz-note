# Year 1 Execution Plan

The first year is about building investing fluency fast enough to produce credible written work.

## Core Priorities

- Learn accounting well enough to judge earnings quality
- Learn valuation well enough to estimate ranges and implied expectations
- Produce 3-5 memos instead of collecting passive notes
- Use engineering skill to support research speed and organization

## Monthly Rhythm

- 1 company deep dive
- 1 annual report summary
- 1 valuation practice exercise
- 1 portfolio or watchlist review
- 1 improvement to research workflow or tooling

## Quarter 1 Focus

- Complete notes in [[topics/finance/accounting-financial-statements|Accounting & Financial Statements]]
- Complete notes in [[topics/finance/valuation|Valuation]]
- Fill [[memos/first-stock-pitch-workspace|First Stock Pitch Workspace]]
- Start a reading archive in [[reading-curriculum|Reading & Curriculum]]
- Follow [[six-month-technical-syllabus|6-Month Technical Syllabus]]

## Output Standard

- Every month should produce at least one reusable artifact
- Every artifact should support either future memos or better decision quality
- Theory without written output does not count as progress
