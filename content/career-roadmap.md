# Career Roadmap

Target role: public-markets fund manager candidate with strong bottom-up research, valuation discipline, and technology leverage.

## Year 1

Theme: build credibility through fundamentals, repeatable research process, and visible work product.

### Quarter 1

- Build statement-reading fluency
- Learn core valuation methods without over-modeling
- Start a weekly reading and note-taking rhythm
- Create the first stock-pitch workspace

### Quarter 2

- Deepen accounting and earnings-quality judgment
- Produce 1-2 full company memos
- Build a simple research dashboard or watchlist workflow
- Start tracking industries and recurring company patterns

### Quarter 3

- Improve thesis quality and variant-perception thinking
- Add portfolio construction basics and sizing logic
- Produce another 1-2 memos with clearer risk framing
- Start reviewing mistakes and open questions systematically

### Quarter 4

- Reach consistent memo quality
- Narrow into 1-2 sectors for deeper familiarity
- Build a small archive of public-quality research
- Prepare for stronger signaling in Year 2

### Year 1 Proof Artifacts

- 3-5 completed investment memos
- 1 sector map or industry note
- 1 repeatable research tooling workflow
- 1 portfolio review process note
- 1 reading archive with concept summaries

## Year 2

- Deepen portfolio construction and risk management
- Publish or archive a consistent body of stock pitches
- Develop sector familiarity and differentiated judgment
- Build a review loop around mistakes and thesis tracking

## Year 3

- Reach professional-level memo quality and idea selection discipline
- Build a visible signal through research, network, and optional credentials
- Be ready for analyst, research, or apprentice-PM opportunities
